WhatsApp pharmacy automation

Medicine requests, handled in seconds

Customers text a medicine name or send a photo of a prescription. Dawaa reads it with AI, checks the stock, reserves it for two hours, and brings in the pharmacist only when a human check is needed.

What it does

One conversation, three possible outcomes.

⚡

Auto reserve

A clear match in the catalog is reserved at once, stock is updated, and the customer gets a confirmation with the total.

🩺

Pharmacist review

Uncertain requests go to the pharmacist on Telegram. One tap to approve or reject, and the customer is told right away.

🔎

Nothing found

If no medicine matches, the customer is politely asked to send the name again or a clearer photo of the prescription.

Main Workflow

Normalizes the incoming message, downloads prescription photos, asks Gemini to read the request, and matches each medicine against the catalog with a fuzzy algorithm.

WhatsApp trigger

Pharmacist decision loop

Requests that need a human check are sent to Telegram. The pharmacist's answer is parsed, the reservation lines and stock are updated, and the customer is notified.

Telegram trigger

Reservation expiry

Every 15 minutes, expired reservations are found and marked, the stock is given back, and the customer is notified.

Every 15 minutes

Low stock report

Every day at 22:00, low-stock medicines are collected into a reorder list and sent to the team by Telegram and email.

Daily · 22:00

Error handling

Any failed run is classified, logged to a sheet, and reported by Telegram and email, so nothing fails silently.
